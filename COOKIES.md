# Cookies Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-18_

This Cookies Policy explains how It's a Car Crash Limited uses cookies and similar tracking technologies in the It's a Car Crash mobile application (the "App"). It should be read alongside our [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md).

It's a Car Crash Limited operates as a mobile-first business and does not currently run a public website. As a result, classic browser cookies are not set by us directly. However, third-party libraries (SDKs) bundled with the App may use cookies, mobile identifiers, advertising IDs and similar technologies — this policy explains what those are and how you can control them.

## What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies and similar technologies are very small text files or pieces of code that often contain a unique identification code. When you use a mobile application, third-party services integrated into that application may ask your device for permission to save such a file on your device and gain access to information. On mobile devices, equivalent technologies include mobile advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA and Google's Advertising ID), software development kit (SDK) identifiers, and local storage used by in-app web views.

Information collected through cookies and similar technologies may include the date and time of use, the features you interact with, your device type and operating system, and information about how you use the App.

## Why do we use cookies and similar technologies?

We — and the third-party services we integrate with — use cookies and similar technologies to:

- keep you signed in to your account;
- remember your preferences and settings;
- keep the App secure and prevent fraudulent or abusive use;
- understand how the App is used and how we can improve it (analytics);
- diagnose crashes and performance issues;
- where you have consented, deliver communications and content that may be of interest to you.

## What types of cookies and similar technologies are used?

- **Necessary** — required for the App to function properly. These include identifiers used to keep you logged in, to remember your preferences (such as language), and to keep the App secure.
- **Performance / analytics** — used to gather statistical information about how the App is used. We use this data for performance and product improvement.
- **Functional** — enable additional functionality, such as embedded media, live chat, or social media sign-in. These may be set by external service providers.
- **Advertising / tracking** — set by external advertising partners and used for profiling and cross-app or cross-site tracking. If you consent to these, we may show advertisements based on your interests, and we may receive aggregated data about how many users have seen or interacted with our advertisements. We do not enable advertising tracking without your consent.

## How can I switch off or remove cookies and tracking?

You can opt out of most non-necessary tracking. If you block all such technologies, certain features of the App may not function fully or as intended.

- **iOS** — under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, you can disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track", and you will also be prompted by each app under App Tracking Transparency (ATT). Under Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising you can disable personalised ads.
- **Android** — under Settings → Privacy / Ads (the exact path depends on your device), you can choose "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation".
- **In-app** — where the App presents a consent prompt, you can change your choices at any time from the App's settings.

If you want to view your consent record, please contact us at [support@itsacarcrash.com](mailto:support@itsacarcrash.com).

## Third-party libraries used by the App

The App does not set cookies of its own. However, it may include third-party code and libraries that use cookies, mobile identifiers, or similar technologies to collect information and to improve their services. These third parties have their own privacy policies that govern their use of that information.

If you would like a current list of the third-party libraries and analytics providers used in the App, please contact us at [support@itsacarcrash.com](mailto:support@itsacarcrash.com).

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in this repository and take effect immediately on posting.
